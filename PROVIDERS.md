# Provider feasibility (reviewed 2 November 2026)

No provider was shown to satisfy the complete brief, particularly an independently safe individualized care plan, approved occasional family use and verified inexpensive ongoing terms. **Infermedica Engine API is the selected integration candidate**, not a claim of a feasible production contract. Its adaptive interview, parse endpoint and separate triage make it the best documented functional fit. ApiMedic is the affordability candidate to revisit after confirming terms and question semantics. All numbers below are published offers, not a purchase or quote.

## Call accounting

For the implemented Infermedica path, one parse plus an initial diagnosis plus one diagnosis for each answer gives **2 + N calls**, or 12 at ten questions, excluding retries and any future triage, explanations, search, metadata or education calls. Trial quota accounting and pricing after trial must be checked against the account terms; the monetary cost cannot be calculated without a quote. For ApiMedic, if each of an initial evaluation and ten updated evaluations is a billable transaction, that is 11 transactions: nominally €0.22 Light or up to €0.44 Premium after free allowance, but paid package minimums are €200/€400 and endpoint accounting and free-tier eligibility remain unverified. These are conditional estimates, not promises of available pricing.

The 80% stopping rule is disabled. A model match or likelihood index should not be presented as a calibrated individual probability. Urgency is separate from ranking. The current application does **not** call triage and cannot recommend a care level.
